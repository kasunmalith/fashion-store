# Demonstration asset provenance

`public/assets/hero.webp` is an original generated demonstration photograph, created using the built-in image-generation tool. It is not an exact photograph of a branded product and makes no claim about any real product specification.

Prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: wide ecommerce hero photographic background, 3:1 landscape. Original premium editorial product photograph of generic unbranded everyday goods on a warm terracotta and dark charcoal studio display: a black mirrorless camera, silver laptop, cream sneaker, folded sage cotton t-shirt and small amber perfume bottle. Arrange objects on right two thirds, left third mostly dark charcoal negative space for white HTML headline overlay. Warm orange late-afternoon rim lighting, realistic surfaces, elegant retail campaign photography, sharp details, no text, no logos, no watermark. This is clearly illustrative demo imagery, not a specific branded product.

The original raster was copied and optimized locally as WebP. Its final project path is `C:\wamp64\www\store\public\assets\hero.webp` in this installation.

`camera.svg`, `laptop.svg`, `phone.svg`, `shirt.svg`, `shoes.svg`, `chair.svg`, `drill.svg`, `perfume.svg`, `rice.svg`, and `battery.svg` are original programmatic product illustrations generated by the optional demo seeder. `placeholder.svg` is a neutral package icon for products with no uploaded image. They are clearly illustrative and may be replaced through product administration.

The small interface icons form one consistent original SVG icon set in `app/View.php`. No external image hotlinks or third-party product logos are used. Northline, Forma, Fieldwork, Forge, Serein, and Harvest are fictional demonstration brands; the store name is editable text branding.
